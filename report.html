Methodology

Risk scores are generated using logistic regression classifiers calibrated to published clinical reference ranges and epidemiological data. Each model outputs a probability estimate (0–100%) representing predicted disorder risk relative to a normative population. Feature contributions are derived from normalized coefficient magnitudes after z-score standardization of input biomarkers.

Risk thresholds: <25% low · 25–50% moderate · 50–75% elevated · >75% high. Primary training dataset: PIMA Indians Diabetes Dataset (Smith et al., 1988; n=768). Reference guidelines: ADA Standards of Medical Care (2024), ATA (Jonklaas et al., 2014), NCEP ATP III / IDF consensus (2009), Endocrine Society (Bornstein et al., 2016).
