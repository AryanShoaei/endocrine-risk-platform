Conditions
Four axes, one model.

Each condition is scored from its own biomarker set against the reference framing of a major clinical guideline.

Thyroid Dysfunction

ATA · Jonklaas 2014
Drivers: TSH, Free T4, Free T3. Rising TSH with low Free T4/T3 pushes the index toward a hypothyroid pattern.

Type 2 Diabetes

ADA Standards 2024
Drivers: fasting glucose, HbA1c, BMI, fasting insulin. Anchored to ADA diagnostic thresholds (HbA1c ≥ 6.5%, glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL).

Metabolic Syndrome

NCEP ATP III / IDF
Drivers: triglycerides, HDL, BMI, glucose. Reflects the atherogenic clustering at the heart of ATP III criteria.

Adrenal Insufficiency

Endocrine Soc. 2016
Drivers: AM cortisol, ACTH, DHEA-S. Low morning cortisol drives the index; the ACTH pattern hints at primary vs. central etiology.
How It Works
From raw labs to a risk band, in five transparent steps.
01

Biomarker input

Twelve clinical values entered via live sliders, each with its reference range.

02

Normalize

Each value is scored against its clinical reference midpoint as a standardized distance.

03

Weight

Hand-specified coefficients combine the relevant biomarkers and symptoms per condition.

04

Transform

A logistic (sigmoid) function maps the weighted sum to a 0–1 index.

05

Stratify

Banded as Low (<25%), Moderate (25–50%), or Elevated (>50%).

✓What EndoRisk is

  • ›An interactive teaching tool that makes the relationship between biomarkers and endocrine risk tangible and explorable.
  • ›A fully transparent, rule-based scoring engine grounded in published clinical reference ranges.
  • ›A self-contained, privacy-preserving web app — every calculation runs locally in your browser.

✕What it is not

  • ›Not a trained or validated machine-learning model, and it reports no diagnostic-accuracy statistics.
  • ›Not a medical device, and not a substitute for clinical evaluation, laboratory interpretation, or a physician.
  • ›Not intended for diagnosis, screening, or any real patient decision-making.

Dashboard Report Methodology
Educational demonstration only. EndoRisk applies transparent, hand-specified scoring rules to user-entered values using published reference ranges (ADA, ATA, NCEP ATP III, Endocrine Society). It does not use trained machine-learning models, was not validated against patient outcomes, and is not a medical device. Do not use it for diagnosis, screening, or clinical decision-making.
