12-Biomarker Live Engine
Four simultaneous logistic regression classifiers update in real time as you adjust clinical biomarkers. Every slider change recalculates all four risk models simultaneously.
🩺
Symptom-Weighted Scoring
30+ clinical symptoms mapped to specific conditions. Checking "cold intolerance" boosts thyroid risk. Checking "polyuria" raises diabetes probability. Symptoms feed directly into the model.
⚡
Intervention Simulator
"What if" modeling — simulate the effect of clinical interventions. Drop glucose by 30 mg/dL, see projected risk reduction across all four conditions in real time.
📋
Publication-Quality Reports
Auto-generated clinical reports with full biomarker tables, radar charts, driver analysis, and clinical narrative. Print directly to PDF for EHR drag-and-drop.
⚖️
Patient Comparison Mode
Load two patient profiles side by side. All four risk models visualized comparatively with grouped bar charts — like a real clinical rounds tool.
What We Model

Four endocrine conditions.
One unified platform.

🫀
Thyroid Dysfunction
Subclinical and overt hypo/hyperthyroidism via TSH, Free T4, and T3 pattern analysis. ATA guidelines (Jonklaas et al., 2014).
TSHFree T4Free T3
🩸
Type 2 Diabetes
Logistic regression on PIMA Indians Dataset (n=768). 78.5% accuracy, 0.84 AUC-ROC. ADA Standards of Medical Care (2024).
GlucoseHbA1cBMIInsulin
⚡
Metabolic Syndrome
NCEP ATP III / IDF consensus criteria. Triglycerides, HDL, BMI, and fasting glucose as composite atherogenic risk factors.
TriglyceridesHDLBMI
🧪
Adrenal Insufficiency
AM cortisol, ACTH, and DHEA-S axis pattern. Endocrine Society clinical practice guidelines (Bornstein et al., 2016).

The Researcher
Aryan Shoaei

Pre-medicine student at the University of Texas at Austin pursuing a B.S. in Biology with a Spanish for Healthcare Professionals Certificate. Conducting independent microbiology research through the Freshman Research Initiative — investigating microbial communities in insects via DNA extraction, PCR, 16S rRNA sequencing, and R-based diversity analysis.

EndoRisk was built as an independent computational research initiative — applying machine learning classification to clinical endocrinology data, bridging wet lab skills with data-driven approaches to metabolic disease risk stratification.
