Model methodology

This platform applies logistic regression models calibrated to published clinical reference ranges and epidemiological data to estimate disorder risk from biomarker inputs. Each model outputs a probability score (0–100%) representing estimated risk relative to a normative population.

Feature contributions are derived from normalized coefficient magnitudes, reflecting the relative influence of each biomarker on the model output. Lab values are normalized to z-scores before coefficient weighting to allow cross-biomarker comparison.

Risk thresholds: <25% low · 25–50% moderate · 50–75% elevated · >75% high. These correspond approximately to the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentile risk profiles in the reference population.
