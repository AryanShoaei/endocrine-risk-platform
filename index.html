Platform capabilities

Built for serious clinical research

Every component reflects real published methodology. This isn't a tutorial project — it's a research tool.

🧬
Multi-model risk engine
Simultaneous logistic regression classifiers for thyroid dysfunction, T2DM, metabolic syndrome, and adrenal insufficiency — all running in real time.
📊
Live biomarker dashboard
Adjust 12 clinical lab values with precision sliders. Risk scores, driver analysis, and lab flags update instantly as you change inputs.
📋
Clinical report generation
Auto-generates a formatted research report from patient data — complete with risk summaries, driver analysis, and clinical recommendations.
📈
Longitudinal tracking
Simulate patient visits over time and visualize how risk trajectories evolve across multiple biomarker readings.
🔬
Feature importance analysis
For every risk prediction, see exactly which biomarkers are driving the score — with normalized coefficient visualization.
📚
Evidence-based references
All thresholds and reference ranges calibrated to ADA, ATA, Endocrine Society, and NCEP ATP III published clinical guidelines.
What we model

Four major endocrine conditions

Each model is calibrated to published clinical criteria and epidemiological reference populations.

Thyroid dysfunction
Detects subclinical and overt hypo/hyperthyroidism using TSH, Free T4, and Free T3 patterns. Reference: ATA guidelines (Jonklaas et al., 2014).
TSHFree T4Free T3
Type 2 diabetes
Logistic regression trained on the PIMA Indians Dataset (n=768). Glucose, HbA1c, BMI, and insulin as primary features. ADA Standards of Medical Care (2024).
GlucoseHbA1cBMIInsulin
Metabolic syndrome
Multi-criteria risk model based on NCEP ATP III / IDF consensus. Triglycerides, HDL, BMI, and fasting glucose as composite risk factors.
TriglyceridesHDLBMIGlucose
Adrenal insufficiency
AM cortisol, ACTH, and DHEA-S pattern analysis. Based on Endocrine Society clinical practice guidelines (Bornstein et al., 2016).
AM CortisolACTHDHEA-S
