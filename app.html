Educational demonstration. EndoRisk applies transparent, hand-specified scoring rules to user-entered biomarker and symptom values using published clinical reference ranges (ADA, ATA, NCEP ATP III, Endocrine Society). It does not use trained machine-learning models, was not validated against patient outcomes, and reports no diagnostic-accuracy metrics. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, screening, or any clinical decision-making.
